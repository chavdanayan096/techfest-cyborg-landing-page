01 · The Fest

Three decades of curiosity, compiled into one festival.

Techfest is the annual science and technology festival of IIT Bombay — and in its 30th edition it stays true to the idea it was built on: give curious minds a place to build, break, compete and begin again.

Every December, the IIT Bombay campus turns into a working prototype of the future. Robots roll beside code sprints, research ideas meet real audiences, and students test what they have built against the best of their peers.

The 30th edition leans into the cyborg idea — not machines replacing people, but people and machines upgrading each other. Bring a prototype, a question, or just your curiosity; the fest supplies the arena.

Across three days you will find competitions that reward engineering nerve, workshops that put tools directly in your hands, and exhibits and talks that show where science and technology are heading next.
